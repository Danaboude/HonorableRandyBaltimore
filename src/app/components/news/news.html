Notes from the Cabinet

Prime Minister Browne Congratulates Randy Baltimore on Emphatic By-Election Victory

Prime Minister the Hon. Gaston Browne has extended heartfelt congratulations to Randy Baltimore on his resounding victory in the 2026 St. Philip's North by-election.

Mr. Baltimore secured an emphatic win, demonstrating the overwhelming confidence and support of the people. The Prime Minister noted that this decisive outcome reflects the electorate's continued trust in the policies and development agenda of the Government.

"The people of St. Philip’s North have spoken clearly and decisively. You now officially take your place among a team committed to delivering progress and prosperity for our nation."
— PM Hon. Gaston Browne
