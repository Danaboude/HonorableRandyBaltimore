About The Honorable

A New Chapter for St. Philip's North

Randy Baltimore is an Antigua and Barbuda Labour Party politician who was elected as the parliamentary representative in the historic 2026 St. Philip's North by-election in a landslide victory.

Though his official swearing-in was momentarily delayed due to a legal challenge regarding his eligibility, the will of the people prevailed. He was officially sworn into the House of Representatives on 27 March 2026.
